How it works

How the predictions are made

The pipeline is written in Python and SQL. The README on GitHub has the full method and the problems fixed along the way.


      

      

        
1Python

          
Data

          
Stats for every player in every Premier League match since 2022-23. This season comes straight from the official
            FPL API, older seasons from Vaastav Anand's open archive.

        
2SQLite

          
Features in SQL

          
Rolling averages of points, minutes, xG and xA, plus how strong the opponent is. Every window ends at
            1 PRECEDING, so a row only knows about games played before that week's deadline.

        
3scikit-learn

          
Model training and testing

          
A gradient boosting model, tuned on 2024-25 and tested on 2025-26, a season left out of training.
            For this season it was retrained on all four.

        
4Every gameweek

          
Team selection

          
Each prediction is multiplied by FPL's chance of playing, so a player listed at 25% keeps a quarter
            of it. The best XI is the highest-predicted legal formation.

Common questions

    

    

      

        
The best XI is the eleven highest-predicted players in a legal formation, with no budget limit.
          It's meant for choosing transfers and a captain, not as a squad to copy.

      

        
The model only sees match stats, so it multiplies each prediction by FPL's own chance of playing.
          The dashboard lists the flagged players and their predictions before the cut.

      

        
After every gameweek. One script pulls the new results from the FPL API, retrains,
          and republishes this site.

      

        
Over 2025-26, a season left out of training, the model's best XI scored about 260 more points than
          the season-average rule. It's ahead this season too, but this early the sample is too small to be sure.
