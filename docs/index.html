How it works

Four steps from raw stats to a pick.

Python and SQL from start to finish. The write-up on GitHub covers the details, including the mistakes I caught along the way.


      

      

        
1Python

          
Collect every match

          
Stats for every player in every Premier League match since 2022-23. This season comes straight from the official
            FPL API, older seasons from Vaastav Anand's open archive.

        
2SQLite

          
Build the features in SQL

          
Rolling averages of points, minutes, xG and xA, plus how strong the opponent is. Every window ends at
            1 PRECEDING, so a row only knows about games played before that week's deadline.

        
3scikit-learn

          
Train and test the model

          
A gradient boosting model, tuned on 2024-25 and tested on 2025-26 without ever seeing it. After passing
            that test it was retrained on all four seasons for this one.

        
4Every gameweek

          
Pick the team

          
Predict the next gameweek, cut flagged players by FPL's chance of playing, and pick the highest-scoring
            legal XI. A player listed at 25% keeps a quarter of his prediction.

          who to bring in or captain, and check prices on the dashboard.

      

        
The model only sees match stats, so it multiplies each prediction by FPL's own chance of playing.
          The dashboard lists the big names who got cut and what they were predicted before.
